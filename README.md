## About me

I’m Manuel Zoccola, a sociologist and anthropologist working on science, technology \& medicine.

My work sits at the intersection of science \& technology studies, the sociology of sport, and food studies, with a focus on how performance-enhancement technologies (supplements, pharmaceuticals, self-tracking) shape the bodies, expectations and life projects of those who use them.
